YOUR WORKSPACE, YOUR WAY

A little help from AI.

To connect AI, copy .env.example to .env, add your OpenAI API key, and restart the server.

OPENAI_API_KEY=your-key-here
OPENAI_MODEL=gpt-4o-mini

The key stays on the local server. AI requests send your resume details and supplied job description to OpenAI and may incur API charges. AI drafts need your review.

Your local storage folder

Keep a copy of your work

Profiles save directly to your local directory. The folder contains workspace.json, the SQLite database, and a database backup. Browser storage is used only to import older data. Profiles have no separate sign-in. Keep this folder when moving or reinstalling the app.
